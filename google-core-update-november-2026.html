What happened

On November 10, 2026, Google released its November 2026 core update. According to the Google Search Status Dashboard, it applies globally and to all languages, and the rollout may take up to two weeks to complete.

What a core update is

Core updates are broad changes to how Google's systems assess and rank pages. They are not aimed at one site or one tactic. Google re-evaluates which pages best answer each search, so some sites move up and others move down, even if nothing on those sites changed.

What to expect over the next two weeks

  • Movement in rankings and traffic. Positions can swing up or down, especially in the first days, and may settle once the rollout finishes.
  • Uneven timing. The rollout is gradual, so different pages and searches change on different days.
  • Local results can shift too. Map pack and organic results are separate, but both can fluctuate during an update.

Details

  • Don't make panic changes. Rewriting pages or changing titles mid-rollout makes it harder to tell what caused a change.
  • Wait for the rollout to finish. Compare performance only after it ends, using a similar period before and after.
  • Check Google Search Console. Look at clicks, impressions and average position for your key pages and queries.
  • Keep improving real quality. Pages that clearly help visitors, show real experience and are kept up to date are what core updates reward.

If your rankings drop

A drop after a core update does not mean your site was penalized or that something is broken. Google's guidance is that there may be nothing to fix; it often reflects other pages being judged more useful. If a drop persists after the rollout is complete, we review the affected pages against the queries that lost ground, check content quality and usefulness, and improve what is weak. Recovery, when it happens, usually comes with later updates rather than immediately.

What NSM Prime is doing for clients

  • Monitoring Search Console data for client sites through the rollout.
  • Recording a baseline so changes can be measured fairly afterwards.
  • Reporting what moved and why, once the rollout is complete.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the November 2026 core update a penalty?

No. A core update is a broad change to how Google ranks pages. It is not a manual action or a penalty against a specific site.

How long will the update take?

Google says the rollout may take up to two weeks from November 10, 2026.

Should I change my website right now?

Generally no. Wait until the rollout is complete, then review the data before deciding on changes.

Will my rankings come back if they drop?

It depends on the pages and the queries. Improving usefulness and quality gives you the best chance, but nobody can guarantee a recovery.
