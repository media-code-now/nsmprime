Reviews and Trust Signals for Restaurants

Diners look for current menus, accurate hours, food photography, accessibility details, and diner feedback. Publish only claims that can be verified. Ratings and testimonials should come from real customers, remain attributable where appropriate, and match the source platform.

Ask for feedback after a completed service or visit without offering incentives for positive sentiment. Respond professionally, protect private information, and use recurring feedback to improve both operations and website answers. Do not reuse a rating or review count across unrelated pages.
