Our Founder's Journey

From Vision to Las Vegas' Premier Digital Marketing Agency

Noam Sadi - Founder & CEO

Digital Marketing Pioneer | Web Development Expert | Las Vegas Business Leader

With over 15 years of experience in digital marketing and web development, Noam Sadi founded NSM Prime Media Group with a simple but powerful vision: to empower Las Vegas businesses with cutting-edge digital solutions and world-class customer service.

Recognizing a gap in the market for personalized, results-driven digital marketing services, Noam built NSM Prime from the ground up. What started as a small team of dedicated professionals has grown into one of Las Vegas' most trusted digital marketing agencies, serving hundreds of clients across diverse industries.
